Our Story
Elevating the Standard of Serviced Living
Founded on a passion for architecture and uncompromising service, Unique Nights was born from a simple observation: the travel industry lacked a middle ground between cold luxury hotels and unpredictable short-term rentals.
We set out to create a portfolio of properties that offered the consistency and high-end finish of a five-star hotel, with the soul, space, and freedom of a private home. Each location in our collection is hand-selected and meticulously renovated to reflect its local character while meeting our strict global standards of comfort.
